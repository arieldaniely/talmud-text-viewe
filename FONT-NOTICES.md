# External open fonts

The viewer requests **Noto Serif Hebrew** and **Noto Rashi Hebrew** from Google Fonts.
No font binaries or original source-edition fonts are distributed in this repository.

Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 by their authors.
Their font licenses are independent of the viewer's personal-use license.

- [Noto Serif Hebrew license](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notoserifhebrew/OFL.txt)
- [Noto Rashi Hebrew license](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/notorashihebrew/OFL.txt)
- [Noto Hebrew project](https://github.com/notofonts/hebrew)
